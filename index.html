About

LaMe is an independent metal project focused on atmosphere, power and raw emotional intensity. The sound is built from distortion, weight and movement — a mix of modern aggression and old-school attitude.

Whether it is a single, a live set or a new demo, the mission stays the same: create music that feels heavy, personal and unforgettable.
